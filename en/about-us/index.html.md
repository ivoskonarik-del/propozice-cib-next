## Long-term support Music that helps

Every year
since
2014

CIB GROUP has long supported the Hippokrates Foundation (Nadační fond Hippokrates). Every year we hold a benefit concert at which Radka, Eva and Emma Španko perform with EvRam Band. During the evening, our business partners and friends also join us in supporting the foundation.

The Hippokrates Foundation supports the care of patients at the Gynaecological Oncology Centre of the General University Hospital in Prague, the training of doctors and nurses, and improvements to the environment in which treatment takes place.

For us, this is not a one-off donation. The concert has become an annual tradition that began in 2014 in the Chapel of the Holy Cross at Apolinář. Supporting the foundation is a long-term commitment for us and a tradition we want to keep building on.

## The building from 1899

Residence Vodičkova 33
The building
from 1899

The group acquired the building in 2017. Construction work on its complete refurbishment began in 2022 and occupancy approval was granted in May 2024. Alongside the group's head office, the building houses prestigious offices for lease and the Vodičkova Luxury Suites apartments.

The herringbone parquet floors, stucco mouldings and tall windows have been preserved. New elements are designed to complement the original layout rather than overwrite it.

## History and milestones Milestones that shaped the group

Select a year to see its milestones.

### Today

- We continue the long-term development of the Obec v zahradě project in Lichoceves and actively look for new opportunities in office, retail and mixed-use properties.

### 2026

- CIB GROUP completes the sale of the Písnice residential portfolio, comprising 16 apartment buildings and 760 apartments, to the WOOD & Company City fund, closing the investment cycle of an asset it had owned and developed since 2017.

- The sale of the residential portfolio in Strašnice and Hostivař is completed.

### 2024

- Pavel Španko becomes the sole owner of the group.

- The group is restructured and linked with the other activities of the family holding.

- The Obec v zahradě project and Emma Sophia Stables become part of the wider family holding.

- Refurbishment of Residence Vodičkova 33 is completed and the building receives its occupancy approval in May 2024.

- The Liberec region residential portfolio and the Benkova apartment buildings change ownership. CIB GROUP continues to manage them.

### 2023

- Refurbishment of Residence Vodičkova 33 continues.

### 2022

- Construction work begins at Residence Vodičkova 33.

- Project preparation for the Písnice site continues.

- Refurbishment of part of the residential portfolio in Hrádek nad Nisou.

- Refurbishment of apartment buildings in Prosek.

### 2021

- The group's portfolios are stabilised during the Covid-19 pandemic.

- Preparation for the extensive refurbishment of Residence Vodičkova 33.

- Development of the urban planning concept for Písnice and preparation for the revitalisation of selected apartment buildings.

### 2020

- Balabenka Office Building obtains BREEAM In-Use Very Good certification.

- Sale of the residential portfolio in Klatovy.

- Sale of the Delta Haus office building.

- Public presentation of the completed urban planning study for Písnice, Prague.

### 2019

- The restructuring of the residential portfolio is completed and the portfolio is transferred to CIB Rental, s.r.o.

- The residential portfolio is refinanced after the restructuring.

### 2018

- Launch of the pisnice.com information portal.

- A client centre opens for Písnice tenants, with facilities for the local management team.

- CIB GROUP moves its head office to the Edison building on Jeruzalémská Street.

- Sale of an office property in Kačerov, Prague.

- Acquisition of a shopping centre in Písnice, Prague.

### 2017

- Acquisition of the Vodičkova 33 building in the centre of Prague.

- Revitalisation of the residential portfolio in Klatovy.

- Acquisition of a residential portfolio of 740 apartments in Písnice, Prague.

### 2016

- Building works at Balabenka Office Building are completed and the building is fully let.

- The Delta Haus refurbishment is completed and the building is fully let.

### 2015

- The office space at Balabenka Office Building is completed and leasing begins.

- Complete refurbishment of the Delta Haus office building and start of leasing.

### 2014

- Gradual sale of stabilised, fully let regional commercial properties, originally owned by Komerční banka, to local investors.

- Acquisition of the Balabenka office building in Prague.

### 2013

- Acquisition of the vacant Delta Haus office building in Vinohrady, Prague.

- Refurbishment of apartment buildings in Chodov and Prosek begins.

### 2012

- Complete refurbishment of apartment buildings in Strašnice and Hostivař.

- Acquisition of 100 apartments in Chodov and Prosek.

### 2011

- Acquisition of an apartment building with 50 apartments in Klatovy.

- Complete refurbishment of apartment buildings in Liberec, partly financed by a European Union operational programme.

- Refurbishment of the office buildings acquired from Komerční banka and their gradual letting to new tenants.

### 2010

- Exit from the construction business.

- Acquisition of 300 residential units in Liberec.

- Acquisition of an office property in Kačerov, Prague.

- Acquisition of seven office buildings from Komerční banka with a partial leaseback.

### 2009

- After the foreign investors leave, the group is taken over by its management.

- The group refocuses its strategy on the real estate market and expands its residential and commercial portfolio.

### 2008

- A new information system is introduced to manage the residential and office portfolio.

- The group becomes a shareholder in INTEGRA Stavby, a.s., formerly Integra Liberec, a.s.

### 2007

- Preparation for the refurbishment of apartment buildings in Hrádek nad Nisou.

### 2006

- Acquisition of a housing stock of 270 apartments in Hrádek nad Nisou at a public auction.

### 2005

- Acquisition of the first residential portfolio in Hostivař and Strašnice, 150 residential units in total.

- Entry into the construction sector through the acquisition of a building materials manufacturer.

### 2004

- CIB GROUP is founded, originally as Canadian Investment Bridge.

## Residence Vodičkova

Built in 1899
Residence
Vodičkova

The historic Vítkův House underwent a complete refurbishment, completed in May 2024. It now brings together prestigious office space, Vodičkova Luxury Suites and the headquarters of CIB GROUP.

Prestigious office space in a sensitively restored historic building in central Prague, just steps from Wenceslas Square. The building's original architectural character is complemented by contemporary technical standards.

## Residence Vodičkova 33 The building inside and out

## Building and courtyard Facade, passageway and inner courtyard

The herringbone parquet floors, stucco mouldings and tall windows have been preserved. The new elements, namely glass partitions with black frames, building services and facilities, are arranged to complement the original layout rather than overwrite it.

The building stands on Vodičkova Street, a few steps from Wenceslas Square, within walking distance of the Můstek and Muzeum metro stations. Elegant common areas and round the clock security. Dining and services within the building and in the surrounding area.

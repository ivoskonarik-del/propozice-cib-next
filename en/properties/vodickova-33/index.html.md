## Residence Vodičkova

Built in 1899
Residence
Vodičkova

The historic Vítek House was completely refurbished, with work completed in May 2024. Today it brings together prestigious office space, Vodičkova Luxury Suites and the head office of CIB GROUP.

Prestigious office space in a sensitively renovated historic building in central Prague, a few steps from Wenceslas Square. The original architectural character blends naturally with contemporary technical standards.

## Residence Vodičkova 33 The building inside and out

## Building and courtyard Facade, passageway and inner courtyard

The herringbone parquet floors, stucco mouldings and tall windows have been preserved. The new elements, namely glass partitions with black frames, building services and facilities, are arranged to complement the original layout rather than overwrite it.

The building stands on Vodičkova Street, a few steps from Wenceslas Square, within walking distance of the Můstek and Muzeum metro stations. Prestigious common areas and 24/7 security. Restaurants and services in the building and its surroundings.
